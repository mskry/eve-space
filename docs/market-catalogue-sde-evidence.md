# Market catalogue SDE evidence

The official Tranquility JSONL archive `eve-online-static-data-3542233-jsonl.zip` (release 2026-09-24) has 2,114 records in `marketGroups.jsonl`. Of `types.jsonl`, 19,561 records are `published: true` and have a non-null `marketGroupID`. Exactly 56 market groups disagree between the boolean `hasTypes` hint and the presence of published direct assignments. These counts are from the archive, not an ESI active-type list. The earlier locally published build 3539543 independently has the same two row counts; it is not the source of the `hasTypes` comparison.

`sde-ingest/market-catalogue-evidence.json` contains reduced records copied from those three official JSONL members, retaining the keys needed to reproduce edge cases. Group 604 (`Micro`) advertises `hasTypes: true` but has no published direct assignments. Group 614 (`Criminal Evidence`) advertises `false` yet has three published direct types and child groups (including 751). Published type 35912 belongs to unpublished inventory group 1324 but is assigned to market group 2332; type 60771 likewise belongs to unpublished inventory group 4161. Group 1659 has no `iconID`. The fixture omits unrelated fields and non-English translations. The counts at the top record the full-archive measurement, not fixture lengths.

The source market group uses `_key`, optional `parentGroupID`, localized `name`/`description`, optional `iconID`, and `hasTypes`. The source type uses `_key`, `groupID` (inventory), optional `marketGroupID`, localized `name`/`description`, and `published`. The catalogue derives direct membership from published type assignments rather than `hasTypes` or inventory-group publication. It retains English display names and the nullable icon identity; inventory category/group identity does not form the market tree. Base price, live orders, active-type coverage, history, and region prices are outside the static catalogue.

## Core-data product

`market-catalogue` is a public, route-only installed-module product. The route must declare it in its contribution; an undeclared route, resource projection, and activity provider receive no method. Its request/result variants share one committed `{ buildNumber, ingestVersion, ingestedAt }` revision: `tree` returns all `{ id, parentId, name, iconId, directTypeCount }` groups with `complete: true`; `group-types` returns at most 100 direct `{ id, groupId, name }` items with an opaque continuation cursor; `search-index` returns every published assigned type's compact ID/group/name record with `complete: true`. Complete arrays are sorted by numeric ID. The product permits at most 4,000 groups and 32,000 types. The tree validates hierarchy and direct counts, the group page queries only the requested bounded slice, and the index checks its full bound and orphaned assignments. Each read happens in one repeatable-read transaction protected from concurrent SDE table replacement. A missing revision, empty group tree, orphaned parent or type, parent cycle, invalid field, bound breach, or database timeout reports unavailable instead of a misleading complete result. PostgreSQL remains the source; the adapter does not call ESI or read live market coverage. See [public delivery and measured sizes](market-catalogue-delivery.md).
